Yamine

Travel, understood.

Yamine is a travel companion app being built by Yamine Ltd. It helps travellers orient in a new country from the first minute: what to know before you go, how things work when you land, and the facts that matter, verified against official sources.

Yamine is in private development and has not launched. App Store and Google Play releases will follow.
