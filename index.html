Yamine

Travel, understood.

Most travel apps show you places. Yamine's job is understanding: arriving in a country and knowing how it works from the first minute - entry rules, money, connectivity, transport, safety, custom - with each fact researched against official sources and carried with its receipt, not scraped and hoped.

Around that core: a globe that holds where you have been and where you want to go, country briefings before you fly, orientation the moment you land, and trips built from what you actually did. Seven languages from the ground up. Private by design: your places, trips and captures are yours, deletion is built to really delete, and nothing about you is sold or shared.

Yamine is in private development and has not launched. App Store and Google Play releases will follow.
