Yamine

Travel, understood.

Most travel apps show you places. Yamine's job is understanding: arriving in a country and knowing how it works from the first minute - entry rules, money, connectivity, transport, safety, custom - with each fact researched against official sources and carried with its receipt, not scraped and hoped.

Know before you go

Country briefings cover the things that actually decide your first day: how you get in, how you pay, how you get online, how you move around, what to watch for and what is simply done differently. Every briefing is written from researched, source-checked facts, and each fact keeps a record of where it came from.

Orient the moment you land

On arrival, Yamine's job is a fast, honest answer to "where am I and how does this place work" - the essentials of any place, understood in under a minute, before you have local data or local instincts.

A globe that holds your travels

Where you have been and where you want to go live on one globe. A travel passport grows out of what you actually did, and trips assemble themselves from the places, notes and captures you collect along the way - planned trips, live trips, and the record they leave behind.

Facts with receipts

Yamine's country knowledge is verified before it reaches you. Claims are researched against official and authoritative sources, graded, and carried with their provenance. Where something cannot be verified, the app says so rather than guessing.

Seven languages, from the foundations

Yamine is built multilingual from the ground up rather than translated as an afterthought, so the understanding travels with you in the language you think in.

Private by design

Your places, trips and captures are yours. Deletion is built to really delete. Nothing about you is sold or shared. The full detail is in our privacy policy.
