The World,
understood.

Wherever you are - a city that is new to you, or the town you have lived in all your life - Yamine helps you make sense of your surroundings: what’s here, what matters, what’s next. The whole globe, at your fingertips.

When it comes to travel: most travel apps show you places. Yamine tells you how a country works - entry, money, connectivity, transport, custom - what you need before you land, and from the minute you do. So you arrive confident, oriented and connected.

Orient

Oriented in your first minute.

Open Yamine anywhere and get a fast, honest answer to “where am I, and what should I know about here?” One minute, and the place makes sense: you step out the door sure of yourself, in a place that felt foreign a moment ago.

Explore

Know before you go.

Open any country and it is already laid out: how you get in, how you pay, how you get online, how you move around, and what is simply done differently - plus a short checklist you can start ticking before you fly. The homework is done; the daydreaming can start early.

Trips

Trips that remember themselves.

Planned trips, live trips, and the record they leave behind. A trip assembles itself from the places, notes and captures you collect along the way - so you can be where you are, making memories, not lists, while the trip keeps its own diary.

Passport

A passport that grows out of what you did.

Where you have been and where you want to go live on one globe. Stamps are earned from real visits - a record of the world as you have actually met it, growing into something worth looking back on: your life, well travelled. Share it, or keep it entirely yours.

Add

And when something catches your eye.

Paste a link from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or a map. Name a place. Drop in a photo, or a note. Yamine reads it and files it where it belongs - a wishlist, a live trip, a city you will reach next year. “Someday” finally has a home.

Checked and dated

Verified before it reaches you.

Yamine’s facts come checked and dated - every country page shows when it was last looked at. And where something is not certain, Yamine says so, plainly - so you always know where you stand.

Every screen comes in light and dark.

Private by design

Yours means yours.

Only what a feature needs The app collects only the data a feature needs in order to work - nothing gathered on spec.

Deletion that deletes Deleting your account deletes the personal data behind it. Deletion is built to really delete.

Not for sale. No ad tracking. Personal data is not sold and is not shared for advertising. No third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
