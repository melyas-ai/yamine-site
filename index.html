Orient

Oriented in your first minute.

Land, open Yamine, and get a fast, honest answer to “where am I and how does this place work” - before you have local data or local instincts. Each reading you take is kept, so the first minutes of a trip become part of its record.

Explore

Know before you go.

Country briefings cover what actually decides your first day: how you get in, how you pay, how you get online, how you move around, what to watch for and what is simply done differently. Each country carries a short checklist you can start ticking before the flight - tick one thing, and the list is yours.

Trips

Trips that remember themselves.

Planned trips, live trips, and the record they leave behind. Trips assemble themselves from the places, notes and captures you collect along the way - with the people you travelled with, and the money you owe each other, in one place.

Passport

A passport that grows out of what you did.

Where you have been and where you want to go live on one globe. Stamps are earned from real visits, not planned ones - a travel record that grows out of what you actually did, ready to share or to keep to yourself.

Add

And when something catches your eye.

Paste a link from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or a map. Name a place. Drop in a photo, or a note. Yamine reads it and files it where it belongs - a wishlist, a live trip, a city you will reach next year.

Facts with receipts

Verified before it reaches you.

Yamine’s country knowledge is researched against official and authoritative sources, and carried with its provenance - each fact keeps a record of where it came from. Where something cannot be verified, the app says so rather than guessing.

Every screen comes in light and dark.

Private by design

Yours means yours.

Only what a feature needs The app collects only the data a feature needs in order to work - nothing gathered on spec.

Deletion that deletes Deleting your account deletes the personal data behind it. Deletion is built to really delete.

Not for sale. No ad tracking. Personal data is not sold and is not shared for advertising. No third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

The full detail, for this site and for the app, is in the privacy policy.
