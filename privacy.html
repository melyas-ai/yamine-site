Privacy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This policy covers the yamine.app website and states the privacy commitments the Yamine app is being built to. The data controller is Yamine Ltd, registered in England and Wales (company number 17383120), registered office 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom.

This website

The yamine.app website is a static informational site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no trackers and has no forms. We do not collect, store or share any personal data through it.

Like any website, the infrastructure that serves these pages keeps basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation. Yamine Ltd does not access or use those logs, and they are not used to identify visitors.

The Yamine app

The Yamine app is in active development and has not yet been released. Before any public release, this policy will be updated with the specifics of what the app processes and why, and the store listings will carry the same information.

The commitments the app is being built to:

  • Minimal collection. The app collects only what a feature genuinely needs to work.
  • Real deletion. Account deletion is built to actually delete your data, not to hide it.
  • No selling of personal data. Your data is not sold, and it is not shared for advertising.
  • No ad trackers. The app is not built on third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Your travel record is yours. Places, trips and captures belong to you and exist for you, not for profiling.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over personal data we hold about you, including access, correction and erasure. Because the website collects nothing and the app is not yet released, there is usually nothing for us to hold; if you believe we hold personal data about you, contact us and we will answer plainly.
